04Process

Four steps. You approve each one.

  1. 1

    Tell me the idea and the product

    The client, the product and the one moment that should stop the scroll.

  2. You approve 2

    I send the direction

    The idea, the hook and the visual look. Nothing moves on until you approve it.

    You get: concept directions and the opening hook

  3. 3

    I build the scenes

    Script, characters, generation, voice and dialect, checked until faces, products and logos hold steady.

    You get: script, characters and locked scenes

  4. 4

    Edit, delivery and a round of revisions

    Editing, sound and captions, then a round of changes on your notes until it is ready to post.

    You get: the final film and caption

Every step is approved before the next.

→Questions

Before you book.

What do I need to send?

A product link or one photo is enough to start, plus a line on who it is for. I ask for the rest at the first step.

Which languages and dialects do you write in?

Arabic in Egyptian, Gulf and Levantine dialects, and English, with one consistent voice across the whole series.

Will the film use a real face or a character?

Characters can be designed from a single photo, and a face, product or logo is locked so it stays the same across a whole series.

How many rounds of revisions are there?

A round of revisions on your notes comes with the final edit.

How long does it take?

It depends on the length and on how many films you need. Put your deadline in the booking form and I will tell you what fits.
